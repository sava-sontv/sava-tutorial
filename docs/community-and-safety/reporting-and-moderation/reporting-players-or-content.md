### How to report another player or user-generated content that violates the community guidelines

If you encounter behavior or content in Savrse that violates our Community Guidelines, you have the right to report it. While in-app reporting tools are planned for future releases, the current version of Savrse does not yet include a direct “Report” button inside the VR App or Community interface.

You can still report issues by contacting our Support team.

**What You Can Report**

You may report any of the following violations:

* Harassment, bullying, or abusive behavior.
* Hate speech or discriminatory language.
* Sexual or NSFW behavior or content.
* Impersonation or identity misuse.
* Dangerous behavior that threatens user safety.
* Inappropriate or restricted UGC (worlds, avatars,, thumbnails, images).

**How to Submit a Report (Current Method)**

Until in-app reporting features are enabled, please follow these steps:

1. Contact Savrse Support (savrsesupport@savameta.com).
2. Include as much detail as possible:

   * Username of the player involved.
   * Name/URL of the world or content.
   * Date and time of the incident (approximate is okay).
   * Description of what happened.
   * Screenshots or video (optional but very helpful).
3. Our team will review the information and take appropriate action based on the Community Guidelines.

**What Happens After You Report**

* The moderation team reviews the incident.
* If a violation is confirmed, actions may include warnings, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans.
* For privacy and safety reasons, we cannot share the outcome of actions taken on another user’s account.
