### Steps to appeal a ban or suspension if you believe it was unjust or you have resolved the underlying issue

If your account was banned or suspended and you believe the action was mistaken or unfair, you may submit an appeal. Appeal review ensures enforcement decisions remain accurate and fair.

**Before You Appeal**

Please read the ban/suspension notification you received. It usually includes:

* The reason for the enforcement action.
* The type of ban (temporary or permanent).
* When a temporary suspension will expire.

If the suspension is temporary and nearing expiry, an appeal may not be necessary.

**How to Submit an Appeal**

Since the current version of Savrse does not provide an in-app appeal tool, please contact Support with the following:

* Your account email and username.
* A clear explanation of why you believe the ban was unjust.
* Any context or evidence that supports your case.
* Confirmation that you agree to follow all Community Guidelines going forward.

Appeals missing essential information may take longer to review.

**Appeal Review Process**

* A moderator re-evaluates the original case.
* Evidence is reviewed carefully and compared with the guidelines.
* The team makes a final decision: approve or reject the appeal.

**Appeal Outcome**

* If approved, your account access will be restored.
* If rejected, the ban or suspension will remain in effect.
* All moderation decisions after appeal review are final.
