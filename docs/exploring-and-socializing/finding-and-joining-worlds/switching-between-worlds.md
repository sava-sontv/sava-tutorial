### How to travel from one world to another in Savrse?

In Savrse VR App, you are always inside a world. There is no “Leave World” option. Instead, you can only switch or travel between worlds by selecting a new destination world.

This guide explains how to switch to different worlds smoothly.

**How to Switch to a New World**

1. Open the Main Menu Panel.
2. Go to the Worlds panel
3. Select any world to open its detail panel.
4. Click Join.
5. Your current world will close, and the new one will load automatically.

**What Happens When You Travel to a New World**

* Your avatar and settings remain unchanged.
* Your previous world session closes automatically.
* If your connection drops, you will come back to the world you joined after reconnecting successfully.

**Notes**

* Switching worlds may take longer depending on world size and your VR device’s memory.
* Some worlds may cause an error if it is unavailable to join.
* You cannot remain in a blank or empty state, you are always placed into a world.
