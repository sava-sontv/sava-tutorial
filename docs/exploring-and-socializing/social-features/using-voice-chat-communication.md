### How to talk with other players in-game and adjust voice chat settings in Savrse

Savrse uses spatial voice chat, allowing you to talk naturally with nearby players in VR. This guide shows how to enable or disable voice chat, adjust settings, and troubleshoot common voice issues.

**How to communicate?**

1. Open the Main Panel inside VR.
2. Navigate to Settings > Audio & Voice.
3. Choose Device is input microphone you are using to speak to others.
4. Adjust Voice slider to hear others' voices in world.

**Adjusting Voice Chat Volume**

1. Go to Audio & Voice Settings.
2. Adjust the following sliders:
   * Voice: Controls how loud other players sound

   * Phone Volume: Controls how loud your voice is for others
3. Make sure the Master Volume slider is not muted.

**Notes**

* Voice chat follows spatial audio rules: players close to you sound louder.
* Some worlds may have world-specific voice zones (quiet areas, muted rooms).
