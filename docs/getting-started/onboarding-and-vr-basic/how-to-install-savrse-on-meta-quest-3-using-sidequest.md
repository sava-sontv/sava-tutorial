## Learn how to download and install Savrse on your Meta Quest 3 using SideQuest.

Savrse can be installed on your Meta Quest 3 through **SideQuest**. Follow one of the methods below to get started.

### Before You Start

Make sure you have:

* A Meta Quest 3
* A USB-C cable to connect your headset to your computer
* A Meta account connected to your headset
* Developer Mode enabled on your Meta Quest 3
* An internet connection

> **Note:** The initial SideQuest setup may require a computer and USB connection.

### Method 1: Install Savrse Directly from SideQuest on Your Headset

This method allows you to browse and install Savrse directly from your Meta Quest 3 after the initial setup.

Step 1: Set Up SideQuest

1. On your computer, go to the **SideQuest Setup** page.
2. Follow the instructions to install SideQuest on your Meta Quest 3.
3. Connect your headset to your computer using a USB-C cable.
4. Put on your headset and select **Allow USB Debugging** when prompted.
5. Select **Always allow from this computer** if you do not want to approve the connection again.

Step 2: Open SideQuest on Your Meta Quest 3

1. Put on your headset.
2. Open **App Library**.
3. Find and open **SideQuest**.

> Depending on your Meta Quest system version, sideloaded apps may appear under **Unknown Sources** or another section for external apps.

Step 3: Find Savrse

1. In SideQuest, search for **Savrse: Multiplayer VR Music World**.
2. Open the Savrse app page.
3. Select **Download** or **Install** to start the installation.

Step 4: Launch Savrse

Once the installation is complete:

1. Open **App Library**.
2. Find **Savrse** in your installed or external apps.
3. Select **Savrse** to launch the app.

You are now ready to explore Savrse on your Meta Quest 3.

### Method 2: Install Savrse Using SideQuest on Your Computer

You can also install Savrse directly from your computer using **SideQuest Advanced Installer**.

Step 1: Install SideQuest Advanced Installer

1. Go to the **SideQuest Setup** page on your computer.
2. Download and install **SideQuest Advanced Installer**.
3. Open SideQuest after the installation is complete.

Step 2: Connect Your Meta Quest 3

1. Make sure **Developer Mode** is enabled on your headset.
2. Connect your Meta Quest 3 to your computer using a USB-C cable.
3. Put on your headset and select **Allow USB Debugging**.
4. Select **Always allow from this computer** if prompted.

Return to your computer and make sure SideQuest shows that your headset is connected.

Step 3: Install Savrse

1. Search for **Savrse** in SideQuest.
2. Open the Savrse app page.
3. Select **Sideload** or **Install to Headset**.
4. Wait until SideQuest confirms that the installation is complete.

Step 4: Launch Savrse

1. Put on your Meta Quest 3.
2. Open **App Library**.
3. Find **Savrse** in your installed or external apps.
4. Select **Savrse** to start the app.

### Having Trouble?

If you cannot install or find Savrse:

* Make sure **Developer Mode** is enabled.
* Check that your Meta Quest 3 is properly connected to your computer.
* Reconnect the USB cable and select **Allow USB Debugging** again.
* Restart your headset and SideQuest.
* Check the external or sideloaded apps section in **App Library** if Savrse does not appear with your regular apps.
