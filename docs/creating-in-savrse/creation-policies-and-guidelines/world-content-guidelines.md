### What type of content is allowed or prohibited in creator-made worlds

Creators must ensure their worlds follow Savrse’s global content standards. These rules exist to keep the platform safe, inclusive, and appropriate for a wide range of ages and cultures.

**Allowed Content**

You may publish worlds that include:

* Social environments, gameplay scenarios, or artistic spaces.
* Mild fantasy themes or non-realistic combat elements.
* Cartoon-style visuals, characters, and animations.
* User-generated art and original 3D assets.

**Prohibited Content**

Worlds must not include:

* Sexually explicit content, nudity, or fetish themes.
* Realistic violence, gore, torture, dismemberment, or self-harm themes.
* Hate symbols, extremist ideology, or discriminatory content.
* Illegal activities (drug production, gambling, weapons trafficking).
* Harassment spaces targeting individuals or groups.
* Content designed to deceive, scam, or harm players.

**Sensitive Content**

If your world contains themes that may be considered sensitive (intense lighting, loud effects, horror elements…), you must clearly label the world using the provided tags.

**Violations**

Worlds that violate policies may be:

* Blocked or unlisted
* Removed from search results
* Suspended until corrected
* Permanently removed
