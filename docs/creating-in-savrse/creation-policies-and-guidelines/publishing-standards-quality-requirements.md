### Technical and quality expectations for worlds published to Savrse

To ensure players have a smooth experience, published worlds must meet minimum performance and usability standards

**Technical Requirements**

Worlds should:

* Maintain acceptable FPS on supported VR devices
* Use optimized models, textures, and lighting
* Avoid excessive particle effects
* Not exceed platform performance thresholds (poly count, draw calls, file size)

**Usability Requirements**

Worlds must:

* Include clear spawn points
* Avoid trapping players in geometry
* Provide intuitive navigation and environment layout
* Not overload users with unnecessary UI or interactions

**World Submission Checklist**

Before publishing, confirm:

* No broken scripts or missing assets
* Lighting builds correctly
* Colliders are stable and consistent
* Audio volume is balanced
* Performance testing completed

**Failure to Meet Standards**

Worlds may be rejected or removed if they cause crashes, severe lag, or contain broken functionality.
