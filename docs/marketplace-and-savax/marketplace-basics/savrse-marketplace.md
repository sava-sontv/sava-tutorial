### Learn how to discover, purchase, and manage digital assets on the Savrse Marketplace

The Savrse Marketplace is where users can discover and purchase digital assets for use within the Savrse ecosystem. Assets purchased from Marketplace can be used to support world creation, creative projects, and other supported experiences in Savrse.

**What You Can Find on Marketplace?**

On the Marketplace, you can browse different types of assets, such as:

* Creator Asset
* Project Template
* In-game Asset

Asset availability may vary depending on creator uploads, review status, and Marketplace policies.

**What You Can Do?**

Users can use the Marketplace to:

* Browse available assets
* Search assets by keyword
* Filter assets by category or type
* View asset details before purchasing
* Check asset price
* Purchase a single asset directly
* Add multiple assets to cart
* Checkout selected assets from cart in one payment
* View seller or shop information
* Access purchased assets and transaction history

**How to Purchase an Asset?**

There are two main ways to purchase assets on Marketplace.

*Option 1: Buy a Single Asset*

To purchase one asset directly:

1. Open the Marketplace page.
2. Select the asset you want to view.
3. Review the asset information, including:
   * Asset name
   * Description
   * Preview
   * Category
   * Technical Detail
   * Price in SavaX
   * Seller information
4. Click Buy Now.
5. Confirm the payment using SavaX.
6. After the purchase is completed, the asset will be added to your Inventory.

*Option 2: Add Assets to Cart*

To purchase multiple assets together:

1. Open the Marketplace page.
2. Select an asset you want to purchase.
3. Click Add to Cart.
4. Continue browsing and add more assets if needed.
5. Open your Cart.
6. Select the assets you want to checkout.
7. Review the total amount in SavaX.
8. Confirm the payment.
9. After the purchase is completed, the selected assets will be added to your Inventory.

**After Purchase**

Purchased assets can usually be found in My Assets or Inventory page.

From Inventory, you may be able to:

* View asset details
* Access purchased assets
* Manage purchased assets for future projects

**For Sellers**

When a user purchases an asset from Marketplace:

* The buyer pays with SavaX.
* The transaction is recorded in the system.
* The seller receives SavaX after the transaction fee is deducted.
* The order can be viewed from Shop Management.

**Important Notes**

* Some assets may require review or optimization before they can be used in certain tools.
* Purchased assets may be subject to creator-defined usage rules.
* Assets removed from Marketplace may still appear in your purchase history, depending on platform policy.
* The final payment amount should be reviewed carefully before confirming checkout.
* Refund availability depends on Savrse Marketplace rules and transaction status.

**Still Need Help?**

If you cannot find a purchased asset or have an issue with a Marketplace transaction, contact Savrse Support (savrsesupport@savameta.com) and include:

* Your Sava account email
* Asset name
* Transaction time
* Screenshot of the issue, if available
