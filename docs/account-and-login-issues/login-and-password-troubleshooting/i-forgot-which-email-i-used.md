Follow these steps may help you solve the problem

**Try to recall:**

* Which email you used when creating your Sava Meta account
* Whether you signed up using a personal, school, or work email

**If you cannot remember your login email, contact Support (savrsesupport@savameta.com) and provide:**

* Possible emails you may have used
* Your display name
* Any previous screenshots or receipts linked to your account

Our team will verify and help you recover the correct email.
