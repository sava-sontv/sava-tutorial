### Reset your password if you can’t log in

If you don’t remember your password, you can reset it anytime.

**How to reset:**

1. Go to the Sava Meta login page.
2. Click Forgot Password
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Check your email for a reset link.
5. Click the link and create a new password.

**Tips:**

* Check your Spam folder if you don’t see the email.
* Reset links expire after a limited time, request a new one if needed.

If you still cannot reset your password, contact Support (savrsesupport@savameta.com).
