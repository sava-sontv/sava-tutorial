### What to do if the reset email doesn’t arrive

If you requested a password reset but didn't receive the email:

**Try these steps:**

* Check the Spam folder if you don’t see the email.
* Make sure you entered the correct email associated with your account.
* Wait up to 5 minutes, email delivery may be delayed.
* Ensure your mailbox isn’t full.

**If the issue continues, contact Support (savrsesupport@savameta.com) and tell us:**

* Your account email
* Approximate time of the reset request
