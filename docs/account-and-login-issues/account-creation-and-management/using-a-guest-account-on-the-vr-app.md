### Learn how Guest Account work and when to use them

Savrse allows you to start exploring instantly using a Guest Account. This option is available only on the VR App and lets you enter the platform without creating an account.

**What Is a Guest Account?**

A Guest Account is a temporary profile created automatically when you click Continue as Guest on the VR App login screen.
It allows you to:

* Join worlds and interact normally
* Access all platform functions on VR App

Guest accounts behave like regular accounts, except they do not have email and password login.

**When Should You Use a Guest Account?**

* When you want to try Savrse quickly
* When you do not want to register yet
* When using a shared or temporary VR device

**Important Notes**

* Guest accounts are stored only on your device.
* If the app is deleted, factory reset, or storage is cleared, your Guest Account may be lost.
* You will occasionally see reminders suggesting you to Link Your Account for safety.

To keep your progress permanently, we recommend linking your Guest Account with an email.
