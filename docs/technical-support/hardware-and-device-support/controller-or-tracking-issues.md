### How to troubleshoot if your VR controllers aren’t responding or if tracking is inaccurate

If your controllers are not responding, drifting, or tracking inaccurately while using the Savrse VR App, try the steps below to restore proper tracking.

**Common Symptoms**

* Controller not detected
* Buttons not responding
* Hand/gesture tracking laggy or inaccurate
* Drifting or floating controllers
* Tracking cuts out in low light or bright light

**How to Fix**

1. **Check Your Controller Battery:**
   Low battery often causes tracking delays or disconnections.
   Replace or recharge the batteries if needed.
2. **Recenter Your Tracking:**
   Use your VR system’s recenter/reset orientation feature to realign your view.
3. **Improve Lighting Conditions:**
   For headsets using optical tracking:

   * Avoid dark rooms
   * Avoid direct sunlight or reflective surfaces
   * Ensure moderate, even lighting
4. **Restart Your VR Headset:**
   This clears temporary tracking glitches.
5. **Clean the Cameras or Sensors:**
   Wipe dust or fingerprints that may block tracking cameras.
6. **Remove Obstructions:**
   Make sure your hands/controllers are visible to your headset’s cameras.
7. **Re-pair Your Controllers:**
   Unpair and pair the controllers again through your device settings.

**Still Need Help?**

Contact Support with your device type, OS version, and controller model.
