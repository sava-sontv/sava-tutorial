If you can’t hear any sound while playing Savrse, such as ambient audio, effects, UI sounds, or other players’ voices, this guide will help you troubleshoot the issue quickly.

1. Check Your Device Volume Settings

* Make sure your VR headset volume is turned up.
* Use the volume buttons on your device to increase the audio level.
* Confirm that your device is not in silent mode.

2. Verify In-Game Volume Settings

Inside Savrse VR App:

* Open the VR Setting Panel.
* Go to Audio & Voice Settings.
* Ensure the following sliders are not set to zero:
  * Master Volume
  * UI Sound
  * World
  * Voices
  * Background Sound
  * Music

3. Restart the VR App

A temporary audio glitch may cause sound to fail.

* Quit Savrse App completely.
* Relaunch the app and rejoin your world.

4. Check Your Headset or Audio Output Device

* Ensure your headphones or built-in speakers are working in other apps.
* If using external headphones, confirm they are fully plugged in or connected via Bluetooth.
* Try switching between speakers/headphones if your device supports multiple outputs.

**Still Having Issues?**

Contact Savrse Support and include:

* Your device model
* App version
* Whether the issue happens in all worlds or only specific ones
