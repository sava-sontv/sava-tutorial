### Overview of the Savrse platform and its core features and experiences

Savrse is a social VR platform where players can explore immersive 3D worlds, choose their avatars, meet friends, and participate in interactive experiences.

**Savrse consists of three main components:**

1. Community

   * Accessed through a browser
   * Allows you to manage your account, browse worlds & avatars, check profiles
   * Mirrors the in-VR panel experience
   * Note: You cannot join or walk inside worlds on the Community
2. VR App

   * The core experience for users
   * Explore worlds in full VR
   * Interact with other players, join friends
   * Browse and change avatar anytime
   * Access similar menus/panels as on Community
3. Creator Hub

   * Accessed through the web (Creator section on Website)
   * Manage your worlds and published project
   * Set world metadata and and link it to a published project
4. Savrse Studio

   * Installed on PC as the main world-building tool
   * Create and manage multiple projects
   * Build 3D environments inside each project (place objects, layout scenes, adjust lighting…)
   * Publish project to Creator Hub to create world

**What you can do in Savrse**

* Browse and choose your avatar
* Explore virtual worlds (VR only)
* Meet and interact with other users
* Manage your social profile & friends list
* Create and manage your own worlds and projects
