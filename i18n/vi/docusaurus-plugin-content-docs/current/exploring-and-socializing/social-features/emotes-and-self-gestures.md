### How to express yourself using gestures, animations, and emotes in Savrse

Savrse provides a set of emotes, gestures, and avatar animations that let you express yourself naturally in VR.

All emotes and animations are triggered through the Action Menu, opened using your VR controller.

**Using Emotes**

1. Open the Action Menu

* Hold Button B on your right VR controller.
* The Action Menu will appear as a circular (radial) menu locked to your right hand

2. Select Emote Section

* Point your right hand toward the Emotes section of the radial menu.
* A panel of emoji-style emotes will appear

3. Select an Emote

* Point and select any emote to show it.

**Avatar Animation Actions**

1. Open the Action Menu

* Hold Button B on your right VR controller.
* The Action Menu will appear as a circular (radial) menu locked to your right hand

2. Select Animations Section

* Point your right hand toward the Animations section of the radial menu.
* A panel of Animation Actions will appear

3. Select an animation 

* Point and select any animation
* Your avatar will play the chosen animation

**Self-Gestures Using Controllers**

Your avatar can perform natural gestures automatically based on your controller movement:

* Wave
* Point
* Thumbs-up / Thumbs-down
* Hand direction gestures
* Head nod / shake

These gestures do not require the Action Menu — they follow your controller’s motion tracking.
