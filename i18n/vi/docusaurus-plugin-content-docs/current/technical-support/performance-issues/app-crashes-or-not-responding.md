### What to do if the Savrse VR App crashes, freezes, or fails to load properly on your device

If the Savrse VR App becomes unresponsive, freezes on a static frame, or stops reacting to input, this guide helps you resolve the issue safely.

**Common Causes**

* Memory overload
* Temporary device freeze
* Corrupted world data
* Network disruptions causing the app to hang

**How to Fix**

* Force-close the app: Quit the Savrse app fully and relaunch it.
* Restart your VR device: This clears cached processes that may cause freezing.
* Re-enter a different world: If a specific world causes freezing, it may be due to world-side issues.
* Check your storage space: Low device storage may cause app instability.

**Still Need Help?**
Contact Support with your device logs (if available) and a description of when the freeze happens.
