### Advice on managing device overheating or quick battery drain when using Savrse for extended periods

If your device becomes hot or the battery drains unusually fast while using the Savrse VR App, this behavior is typically related to device workload, environmental temperature, or general hardware limitations when rendering 3D worlds in VR

**Common Causes**

*   Long play sessions without breaks
*   Rendering high-complexity worlds
*   High device brightness
*   Running background apps consuming power
*   Poor ventilation causing heat buildup
*   Battery health degradation on older devices

**How to Fix**

1.  Take a short break:
    Close the Savrse VR App and allow the device to cool down before continuing.
2.  Improve ventilation:
    Avoid covering the device vents and do not play in hot environments.
    Allow airflow around your device or headset.
3.  Lower device brightness:
    High brightness increases heat and battery usage.
4.  Close background apps:
    Other running applications may drain battery or cause overheating.
5.  Use less demanding worlds:
    Switching to less complex or less crowded worlds can reduce device load.
6.  Restart your VR device:
    Helps reset background processes and stabilize temperature and battery usage.
7.  Charge using an original or certified charger:
    Unstable charging can cause heat and fast battery drain while playing.

**Still Need Help?**

If overheating or fast battery drain continues across multiple sessions, contact Savrse Support and include your device model and approximate play duration before issues start.
