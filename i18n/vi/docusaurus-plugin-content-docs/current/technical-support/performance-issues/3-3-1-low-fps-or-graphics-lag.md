### Tips to improve frame rates and reduce graphical lag or stutter during gameplay

If your VR experience feels choppy, laggy, or the frame rate drops suddenly, these issues are usually related to device performance, world complexity, or background processes.

**Common Causes**

* Running too many apps in the background
* High-render worlds or large crowd environments
* Device overheating
* Low available memory

**How to Fix**

* Close unused apps: Free up system resources by closing any apps running in the background.
* Lower graphics or visual effects: Reduce optional visual settings in the VR App to stabilize performance.
* Move to a less crowded world: High-player environments may impact FPS depending on device capability.
* Restart your VR device: A full device reboot helps clear memory and reset performance.

Contact Support your device model and the name of the world where FPS drops occur.
