### Fix issues when the VR App fails to connect on startup

You may see messages such as “Connection Lost”, “Fail to reconnect”, or be stuck at the loading screen.

**Possible Causes & Fixes**

* Weak or unstable Wi-Fi
   → Restart router or switch to a more stable network.
* VPN or firewall blocking traffic
   → Disable VPN or whitelist Savrse.
* Server maintenance or temporary outage
   → Try again after a few minutes.
* Corrupted local data
   → Restart the VR App fully (force quit and reopen).

**Tip**

If the error continues, try using mobile hotspot to test if the issue is related to your home network.
