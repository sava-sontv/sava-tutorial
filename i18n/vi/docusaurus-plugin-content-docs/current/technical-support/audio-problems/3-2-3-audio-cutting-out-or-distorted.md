If your sound works but frequently drops, stutters, or becomes distorted, it may be related to performance limitations, network instability, or device overheating.

**Common Causes**

* Network latency affecting voice chat
* Device CPU load affecting audio rendering
* Faulty headphones or unstable Bluetooth connection
* App running too long without restart

**How to Fix**

* Check your internet stability: Voice chat relies on a stable connection. Switch to a stronger Wi-Fi network if possible.
* Close unused apps running in the background: Freeing up device resources may improve audio processing.
* Reconnect your headphones: Re-seat wired headphones or disconnect/reconnect Bluetooth devices if applicable.
* Restart your VR device: A device reboot often resolves audio distortion caused by long usage sessions.

**Still Need Help?**

Please contact Support with details about when the distortion occurs and whether it happens only in specific worlds.
