### Guidance on setting up supported VR devices and solving common hardware compatibility issues

This article helps you verify whether your device is supported and guides you through the basic setup required to run Savrse properly.

**Supported Devices**

Currently supported:

* Meta Quest 3S
* Meta Quest 3
* Meta Quest Pro

**Minimum Requirements**

* Stable internet connection
* Updated device firmware
* Sufficient storage for the Savrse VR App

**Setup Checklist**

1. **Update Your Device Software:**
   Ensure your VR headset is running the latest OS version.
2. **Download the Latest Savrse App Version:**
   Install via the official store or your device’s app library.
3. **Allow Required Permissions:**
    Savrse needs access to:

   * Microphone (for voice chat)
   * Storage (for caching and avatar data)
   * Tracking (for VR spatial interaction)
4. **Check Storage Space:**
   Ensure you have enough free space for updates and world data.
5. **Connect to a Stable Network:**
   A strong Wifi 5 or Wifi 6 connection is recommended.

**If Your Device is Not Supported Yet**

You may still follow future updates and announcements for additional device support.
