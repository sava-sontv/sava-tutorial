### General tips for resolving display or sensor problems with your VR headset while using Savrse

If your VR headset has display issues, tracking delays, random disconnects, or glitches while running Savrse, follow these troubleshooting steps.

**Common Problems**

* Blurry or double vision
* Tracking jitter
* Headset not detected
* Random disconnects
* Black screen or frozen view

**How to Fix**

* Adjust Your Headset Fit: Make sure the headset is aligned properly with your eyes to avoid blur.
* Clean the Lenses: Use a microfiber cloth to remove smudges or dust.
* Restart Your Headset: Many display issues resolve after a full reboot.
* Check Tracking Cameras:  Ensure the environment has good lighting and no reflective objects.
* Update Your Device Firmware: Headset manufacturers frequently fix display and sensor bugs via updates.
* Check for Overheating: If the device is too hot, tracking and performance may degrade, let the headset cool, then retry.
* Disable Experimental Features: Some experimental device settings can cause instability in VR apps.

**Still Having Problems?**

Send your device model, firmware version, and a short description of the issue to Savrse Support.
