### An overview of the rules and etiquette that all players should follow to keep the community friendly and safe

Savrse is a social VR environment where players interact closely with each other.

To keep the community safe and enjoyable, all users must follow the Code of Conduct.

This guide outlines the behaviors that are expected, encouraged, and prohibited.

**Core Behavior Expectations**

* Treat all players with respect.
* Be mindful of personal boundaries in VR.
* Use voice and gestures responsibly.
* Follow world rules set by creators or event hosts.
* Use emotes, animations, and gestures appropriately.

**Prohibited Behaviors**

The following actions are **not allowed** anywhere in Savrse:

1. Harassment or Bullying:
 Includes verbal abuse, stalking, intimidation, or targeted harassment.

2. Hate Speech or Discrimination:
 No language or actions that promote racism, sexism, homophobia, or any form of discrimination.

3. Sexual Content or Misconduct:
 No sexual behavior, inappropriate gestures, or unwanted intimacy in VR.

4. Disruptive Behavior:
 Spamming voice chat, playing loud audio, or interrupting events.

5. Impersonation or Deception:
 Pretending to be staff, streamers, or other users.

6. Unauthorized Promotions:
 No advertising, scams, or suspicious links.

**Expected Community Values**

* Be welcoming to new players.
* Encourage positive interactions.
* Help others understand VR controls and safety settings.
* Report bad behavior when needed.

**Notes**

* Violations may lead to warnings, mutes, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans.
* The Code of Conduct applies to all worlds, including publish and unpublish ones.
