### Guidelines for respectful communication and information on how harassment or hate speech is handled in Savrse

This article explains how to communicate responsibly in voice chat and text chat, and what counts as harassment in Savrse

**Chat Etiquette Guidelines**

1. Use respectful language:

   Avoid insults, profanity directed at others, or mocking behavior.

2. Keep voice chat clean and comfortable:

* Avoid shouting or screaming into the mic.
* Do not play loud music or background noise.
* Respect that some users may prefer a quieter experience.

3. Be mindful in crowded worlds:

   Speak in a normal volume tone and avoid dominating conversations.

**Harassment Policy**

Savrse defines harassment as:

* Repeated unwanted interactions
* Following or stalking users around worlds
* Targeting someone with insults or bullying
* Sexual comments or gestures
* Mimicking or mocking disabilities
* Boxing users in or violating VR personal space

**What Happens When Harassment is Reported**

* Moderators review the report.
* Evidence is evaluated (logs, metadata, world context).
* Actions may include:
  * Warning

  * Temporary mute

  * Temporary suspension

  * Permanent ban
