### Learn about SavaX, the virtual currency used for asset purchases on Savrse Marketplace

**SavaX** is the virtual currency used in the Savrse ecosystem. At the current stage, SavaX is used to purchase digital assets on the Savrse Marketplace.

SavaX helps support asset trading between buyers and creators within Savrse.

**What SavaX Is Currently Used For?**

Currently, SavaX is used for:

* Purchasing assets on the Marketplace
* Completing Marketplace checkout payments
* Paying for selected assets in cart

At this stage, SavaX is mainly used for Marketplace asset transactions.

**How SavaX Works on Marketplace?**

A typical Marketplace transaction works like this:

1. A seller lists an approved asset on Marketplace.
2. A buyer purchases the asset using SavaX.
3. The payment is processed by the system.
4. The purchased asset is added to the buyer’s Inventory.
5. The seller receives SavaX after the transaction fee is deducted.
6. The transaction record is saved in the system.

**Where You Can Use SavaX?**

Currently, SavaX can be used in Marketplace to:

* Buy digital assets from creators and shops.
* Pay for multiple selected assets in one transaction.

In the future, there will be more features for users to utilize SavaX.

**Checking Your SavaX Balance**

You can check your SavaX balance from your wallet in My Account page.

Your wallet may show:

* Current SavaX balance
* Transaction history
* Purchase records
* Payment status

**Transaction History**

Your transaction history helps you track:

* Assets purchased
* Amount of SavaX spent
* Transaction date and time
* Transaction status
* Seller or shop information
* Transaction fee information, if applicable

**Future Uses of SavaX**

In future phases, SavaX is planned to support more creator economy features, such as:

* World Donation:
  Users may be able to donate SavaX to world owners after joining a world through the VR App or PC App.
* Creator Payout:
  Content creators may be able to convert or withdraw eligible SavaX earnings into real-world currency through a payout mechanism.

These features may be introduced gradually and may be subject to platform rules, regional availability, verification requirements, and payout policies.

**Important Notes**

* SavaX is currently used for Marketplace asset purchases.
* SavaX transactions may not be reversible once completed.
* Sellers receive SavaX after applicable transaction fees are deducted.
* Some transactions may require additional review if suspicious activity is detected.
* Always review asset details and total payment amount carefully before confirming a purchase.

**Still Need Help?**

If you have an issue with your SavaX balance or transaction history, contact Savrse Support (savrsesupport@savameta.com) and include:

* Your Sava account email
* Transaction ID, if available
* Asset name, if related to a purchase
* Screenshot of the issue
* Approximate transaction time
