### How to explore the world collection and join a world to start playing

Savrse allows you to browse and explore a variety of virtual worlds.

You can browse worlds from both Community and VR App, but joining a world is only possible on the VR App.

**Browsing Worlds**

You can explore worlds through several discovery surfaces:

* **Recently Joined Worlds** – Worlds you recently entered while using the VR App.
* **Recommend** – Personalized suggestions based on your play history and interests.
* **Top Trending** – Worlds that are currently rising in popularity.
* **Popular World** – High-engagement worlds frequently visited by the community.
* **Favorite World** – Worlds you have marked as favorite for quick access.

**How to Join a World (on VR App)**

1. Select any world to open its preview on right panel.
2. Review the world information (name, description, tags, player count).
3. Press **Join**.
4. The world will begin loading, and you will be transported directly into it.
