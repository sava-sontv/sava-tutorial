### Creator responsibilities to maintain safe, respectful environments inside worlds

Creators play a key role in maintaining the community’s safety. Worlds must not enable harmful behavior or create unsafe player experiences.

**Required Safety Standards**

Creators must ensure:

* Worlds do not encourage harassment or bullying.
* No forced interactions target or harm players.
* No misleading or malicious scripts.
* No hidden traps or systems that intentionally violate user expectations.
* Player privacy is fully respected.

**Moderation Responsibilities**

Creators are expected to:

* Respond to feedback regarding unsafe or harmful content
* Update worlds that violate guidelines
* Remove or adjust problematic assets as needed
* Cooperate with Savrse moderation when requested

**Violation Consequences**

Safety violations may lead to:

* Forced content updates
* Temporary block
* Permanent world deletion
* Account warnings or suspension
