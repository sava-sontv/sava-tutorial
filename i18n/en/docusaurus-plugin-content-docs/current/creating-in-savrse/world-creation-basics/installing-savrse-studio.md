### How to download and set up the Savrse Studio tool on your PC for building virtual worlds

Savrse Studio is the desktop creator tool used to build environments, interactions, and gameplay elements for your Savrse worlds.

**System Requirements**

* Minimum:
  * OS: Windows 7 SP1+ (64-bit)
  * CPU: SSE2 (Core 2 Duo / i3-2100)
  * RAM: 4GB
  * GPU: DX10 (GT 8600 / HD 2600 / HD 3000, 512MB)
  * Storage: 10 GB HDD
* Recommended:

  * OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit
  * CPU: Quad-core 2.5GHz+ (i5-4xxx / Ryzen 3)
  * RAM: 8GB
  * GPU: DX11 (GTX 750 Ti / RX 550, 2GB)
  * Storage: SSD 256GB+

**How to Install**

1. Visit website **[https://savrse.savameta.com/creator](https://savrse.savameta.com/creator)**
2. Click “Download Savrse Studio”
3. Download the latest version of Savrse Studio (PC installer).
4. Run the installer and follow the setup instructions.

**Logging In**

* Open Savrse Studio.
* Click Sign in with your browser and using the same Sava Meta account you use for Community / VR App (no separate creator account)
* Click "Open Creator" on your browser to launch the desktop app.
* Once logged in, you will see your Projects and Templates.

**Troubleshooting Installation**

* If the installer fails, disable antivirus temporarily.
* Ensure you have administrator rights.
* Restart and reinstall.
* Contact Support (savrsesupport@savameta.com) if the issue persists.
