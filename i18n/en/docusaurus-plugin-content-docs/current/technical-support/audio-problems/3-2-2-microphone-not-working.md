If other players cannot hear you, your microphone may be disabled, misconfigured, or blocked by system permissions. Follow the steps below to fix the issue.

1. Check Microphone Permissions

Your VR device may be blocking Savrse App from accessing the microphone.

On standalone VR headsets:

* Go to Device Settings → Permissions → Microphone.
* Ensure Savrse is allowed to use the mic.

2. Verify In-Game Voice Settings

Inside Savrse:

* Open the VR Setting Panel.
* Go to Audio & Voice Settings.
* Check that:
  * Device is Headset Microphone Device

  * Phone Volume is not set to zero

3. Check Your Hardware

* Ensure no physical mute switch is turned on (some headphones have one).
* Try a different microphone or headset (if supported).
* Clean your microphone port to remove dust or debris.

4. Network Restrictions

Some networks may block voice services.
Try switching to a different Wifi network or hotspot.

**Still Not Working?**

Please contact Support with:

* Device model
* App version
* Whether your mic works in other apps
