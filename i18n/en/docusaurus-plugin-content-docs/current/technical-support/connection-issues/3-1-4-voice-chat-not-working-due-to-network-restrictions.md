Voice chat may stop working even when you can still move and interact.

**Common Reasons**

* NAT type restrictions
* School or office networks blocking voice ports
* VPN or firewall interference
* Temporary voice server issue

**How to fix?**

* Switch to a home Wifi network
* Turn off VPN
* Restart the VR App
* Try rejoining the world

If using a managed network (school, workplace), voice chat may not be allowed.
