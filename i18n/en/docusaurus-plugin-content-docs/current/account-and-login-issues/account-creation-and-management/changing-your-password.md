### Need to update your login password? Here’s how.

You can update your password at any time from My Account section, follow these step

**Change Password**

1. Open **[Savrse](https://savrse.savameta.com/user/)** in website
2. Click on your avatar in the upper right corner of the screen
3. Select "My Account"
4. Select Change Password
5. Enter fields: Old password, New password, Confirm password
6. Click “Save” to change password

Tip: We recommend using a strong password with at least 8 characters, including numbers and characters.
