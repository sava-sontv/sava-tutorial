### Step-by-step guide to sign up for a new Sava account

Creating a Sava account allows you to access your profile, customize your avatar, join worlds in VR, and connect with friends.

**How to Create an Account**

1. Open the Community site or launch the VR App.
2. Select “Sign up”.
3. Enter your Email
4. Complete the required fields and accept the Terms of Service & Privacy Policy.

**Verify Your Email**

* After signing up, you will receive a verification email.
* Click “Activate Account” to activate your account.

**Troubleshooting**

* Didn’t receive a verification email? Check spam folder.
* Email already in use → Your account may already exist; try password reset.
