### Understanding what’s allowed, restricted, and prohibited in Savrse

Community Standards help clarify the line between acceptable and unacceptable behaviors in different contexts.

**Allowed**

* Friendly conversations
* Casual roleplay
* Dancing, emotes, light humor
* Recording or streaming in public worlds (if permitted)

**Restricted**

* Recording conversations in private worlds without consent
* Loud behavior in shared spaces
* Persistent roleplay that disrupts others
* Adult themes (must follow world rules)

**Strictly Prohibited**

* Threats of violence
* Sexual content involving public spaces
* Misusing avatars to impersonate minors or authority figures
* Attempts to bypass bans or restrictions

**Notes**

* World creators may add additional rules inside their worlds.
* Savrse staff may take action even if behavior happens in private sessions.
