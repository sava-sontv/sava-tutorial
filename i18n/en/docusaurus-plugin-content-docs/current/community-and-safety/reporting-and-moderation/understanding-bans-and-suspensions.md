### Explanation of why accounts may be banned or suspended and how the enforcement process works

Savrse enforces bans and suspensions to maintain a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment. This article explains why enforcement actions happen and what each action means.

**Why an Account Might Be Suspended or Banned**

Common violations include:

* Severe harassment or repeated abusive behavior.
* Hate speech or discrimination.
* Uploading explicit, violent, or illegal content.
* Circumventing safety systems or attempting account compromise.
* Creating or sharing harmful contents.
* Using exploits, third-party hacks, or unauthorized tools.

**Types of Enforcement Actions**

1. Warning:
 A notification informing the user that their behavior violated guidelines.

2. Temporary Suspension:
 The account is restricted for a limited period (e.g: 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days…), depending on severity.

3. Permanent Ban:
 The account is permanently blocked from accessing Savrse and all associated services.

**How Enforcement Decisions Are Made**

* Moderators review evidence or user reports.
* Severity, frequency, and intent are evaluated.
* Decisions follow the Savrse Community Guidelines and Moderation Policy.

**Can I Know Who Reported Me?**

No. All reporting and moderation processes are confidential to protect user privacy.
